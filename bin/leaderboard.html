How points work

Every way of helping AetherSDR earns points: discussing, reporting, fixing, reviewing and merging. Points depend on the kind of action, never on its size, so a longer comment or a bigger PR earns nothing extra. Breaking main costs points, and costs the reviewer who approved the change the most: PRs can be fast and rough, reviews should be strict.

Who is ranked

  • Community contributors and org members are ranked and can be named contributor of the week.
  • The maintainer is scored the same way, penalties included, and shown in the standings, but can't win.
  • Bots (the AetherClaude agent, Dependabot, Copilot) aren't ranked. Their PRs still count for the people who review and merge them.

Earning points

Breaking main

  • A break is the first failed CI or Full Suite run on main after a green one, charged to the PR whose commit it ran on.
  • While main is already red, a later PR is a break too if it makes something new fail: a test that hasn't failed since main went red. Merging into a red main without breaking anything further costs nothing.
  • Runner problems (out of disk or memory, timeouts, a lost runner) and failures that pass when re-run don't count.
  • Only approvals of the code that was actually merged count. An approval of an earlier push, or one GitHub dismissed, doesn't.
  • Someone with several roles in the same PR (say, author and merger) takes only their largest penalty.

Fair play

  • Comments need at least 15 characters and more than "+1", "thanks" or "LGTM".
  • Comments score at most once per person per thread per day, and at most 10 comments a day score.
  • Replies on your own PR score at most twice per PR.
  • One review per reviewer per PR per day scores (the strongest one that day). Replies in a review thread count as comments, not new reviews, and a review covers your other comments on that PR the same day.
  • At most 4 new issues per person per day score.
  • Issues and PRs labelled spam or invalid lose their points.

Contributor of the week

A week runs from one AetherSDR release to the next. When a release is published, the top-scoring eligible contributor for that week is named contributor of the week. Until then the week's leader is shown as "leading". Ties go to more PR points, then more review points.
