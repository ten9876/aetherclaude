How points work

Every way of helping AetherSDR earns points: discussing, reporting, fixing, reviewing and merging. Points depend on the kind of action, never on its size, so a longer comment or a bigger PR earns nothing extra. Breaking main costs points, and costs the reviewer who approved the change the most: PRs can be fast and rough, reviews should be strict.

Who is ranked

  • Community contributors and org members are ranked and can be named contributor of the week.
  • The maintainer is scored the same way, penalties included, and shown in the standings, but can't win.
  • Bots (the AetherClaude agent, Dependabot, Copilot) aren't ranked. Their PRs still count for the people who review and merge them.

Earning points

There are two separate scores. Every action counts toward exactly one of them.

How points stack: from issue to merged PR

Points add up along the way, and several people earn from the same fix. Follow one bug through:

Breaking main

  • A break is the first failed CI or Full Suite run on main after a green one, charged to the PR whose commit it ran on.
  • While main is already red, a later PR is a break too if it makes something new fail: a test that hasn't failed since main went red. Merging into a red main without breaking anything further costs nothing.
  • Runner problems (out of disk or memory, timeouts, a lost runner) and failures that pass when re-run don't count.
  • Only approvals of the code that was actually merged count. An approval of an earlier push, or one GitHub dismissed, doesn't.
  • Someone with several roles in the same PR (say, author and merger) takes only their largest penalty.

Fair play

  • Comments need at least 15 characters and more than "+1", "thanks" or "LGTM".
  • Comments score at most once per person per thread per day, and at most 10 comments a day score.
  • Replies on your own PR score at most twice per PR.
  • One review per reviewer per PR per day scores (the strongest one that day). Replies in a review thread count as comments, not new reviews, and a review covers your other comments on that PR the same day.
  • At most 4 new issues per person per day score, and at most 15 issue closes a day.
  • Issues and PRs labelled invalid earn nothing for being opened. Those labelled spam earn nothing and cost 3 points.

Steward of the week

A lot of what keeps AetherSDR moving isn't writing PRs: reviewing them, merging them, triaging and cleaning up issues, finishing other people's PRs and cutting releases. That work earns steward points, a separate score from contributor points: no action counts toward both. Steward of the week ranks steward points (less the approver and merger penalties); contributor of the week ranks contributor points (less the author's penalty). A steward is top contributor only if their contributor points alone put them there. Switch the table between Contributor and Steward to rank by either.

Backer of the week

Financial support on Open Collective earns 10 backer points per $1, a third score of its own. Backer points count in the Total ranking and name a backer of the week, but never count toward contributor or steward of the week, so a donation can't outbid code or reviews. Donors appear under their Open Collective name, or on their GitHub row when the two are linked.

Badges

  • 1st: did something for the first time ever that week (first issue, comment, PR, merged PR, review, donation…). Hover it to see which. Welcome!
  • Core dev team.
  • BACKER supports AetherSDR on Open Collective.
  • MAINTAINER scored and shown, but can't win.

Contributor of the week

A week runs from one AetherSDR release to the next. When a release is published, two people are named for that week: the contributor of the week (most contributor points) and the steward of the week (most steward points). The same person can win both, but only by topping each score on its own. Until the release, the week's leaders are shown as "leading". Contributor ties go to more PR points, then more issue points; steward ties to more review points.
